# Roamwise

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web

## Stack
User specified HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Node.js, Vercel Functions, Gemini, Geoapify, Leaflet, Open-Meteo, and localStorage. No framework or database.

## Users and purpose
Tourists planning trips and locals finding nearby outings. A Grade 12 Computer Science project with presentations beginning November 5, 2026. Success means a useful, runnable travel discovery app with real places, usable maps, persistent favorites, and geographically coherent plans.

## Brand commitments
Roamwise. White/off-white surfaces, charcoal type, muted teal/blue, large elegant headings, destination photography, whitespace, rounded cards, restrained motion. User references Airbnb and Google Travel as quality targets. User explicitly instructed development to continue without another design decision round.

## Product principles
- Actual retrieved places only; AI cannot add places or facts.
- Missing prices, hours, photos, and forecasts stay explicit.
- Planning works when Gemini fails.
- Secrets stay on the backend; no publication without approval.

## Capabilities
Tourist/local search, manual/geolocation inputs, interests, budget, pace, weather, details, maps, favorites, selected or automatic multi-day itineraries, saved plans, and directions.
